## 2. Park-level DM&R for FY2019, FY2020 and FY2021: not available

**What it is.** Park-by-park DM&R for these three fiscal years.

**Where and when it was last published.** We found no publication. The NPS
reporting page said reports were available for FY2014 through FY2018 in
captures from 2020-06-24 through 2022-01-28 (for example
https://web.archive.org/web/20220128090604/https://www.nps.gov/subjects/infrastructure/identifying-reporting-deferred-maintenance.htm).
The next park-level release, captured 2022-06-12, was labeled Fiscal Year 2022
Quarter 1 (item 3). NPS-wide totals for FY2019-FY2021 ($13.131 billion,
$14.851 billion, $23.745 billion) appear in CRS Report R48136, sourced to "CRS
communication with NPS Office of Legislative and Congressional Affairs, July
27, 2023":
https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/2024-07-25_R48136_b8f735eff6a455e0ad5cff787c7348b59f659b10.html

**Where it is absent now.** Not on https://www.nps.gov/subjects/plandesignconstruct/defermain.htm
or https://www.doi.gov/deferred-maintenance-and-repair (2026-10-22). Not in the
Wayback index of `nps.gov/subjects/infrastructure/upload/*` or
`nps.gov/common/uploads/sortable_dataset/infrastructure/*` (queried
2026-10-22). Not in CRS R43997 (2023-08-08), CRS R48136 (2024-07-25 and
2026-10-15), or the DOI OIG report 2020-CR-066 (September 2023), which give
national figures only.

**Evidence.** The archived pages above. Because we cannot show these figures
were ever published, they are labeled "not available."

## 7. Park-level location counts: not available

**What it is.** The number of "locations" (assets) behind each DM&R figure.

**Where and when it was last published.** The current page gives "Count of
Locations" by region for FY2023-FY2025 only
(https://www.nps.gov/subjects/plandesignconstruct/defermain.htm, 2026-10-22).
Park fact sheets give asset counts by category (buildings, trail miles and so
on) but not a location count. We found no park-level location count in any
source, so `locations` is empty in the processed file.

# Availability: park budgets (NPS budget justifications)

Checked 2026-10-22. Facts only. We do not know why any item is missing, and
we make no guess.

## 1. FY 2026 NPS budget justification: not available

- **What it is.** The NPS budget justification ("Green Book") for FY 2026.
  If it followed past books, it would have had the park table with FY 2024
  park base amounts.
- **Last published.** The last NPS book before FY 2027 is FY 2025:
  https://www.doi.gov/sites/default/files/documents/2024-03/fy2025-508-nps-greenbook_2.pdf
  (linked from https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/budget.htm in Wayback captures from
  20240425090851 on).
- **Where it is absent now.**
  - https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/budget.htm (checked 2026-10-22) lists FY 2027,
    then FY 2025 back to FY 2010. It has no FY 2026 entry.
  - https://www.doi.gov/budget/appropriations/2026 (HTTP 200, checked
    2026-10-22) links 18 FY 2026 bureau justifications (BLM, BOEM, BSEE,
    OSMRE, BOR, CUPCA, USGS, FWS, BIA, BIE, OS-DWP, OIA, SOL, OIG, BTFA,
    NIGC, USWFS, NRDA). None is for NPS.
  - Guessed file names (`nps-2026-greenbook508.pdf`,
    `nps-2026-budget-justification508.pdf`) in each doi.gov upload folder from
    `/documents/2025-05/` to `/documents/2026-03/` all returned HTTP 404.
- **Evidence.**
  - None of 11 Wayback captures of the DOI FY 2026 page, from 20250604050817
    to 20260608091429, lists an NPS justification. The number of bureau
    entries grew from 0 (June 2025) to 19 (February 2026 on).
  - None of 31 Wayback captures of the NPS budget page, from 20240425090851
    to 20261009110057, mentions FY 2026.
  - CRS report R42757, "National Park Service Appropriations: Ten-Year
    Trends," updated July 6, 2026, footnote 5: "NPS did not release a budget
    justification for FY2026."
    (https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R42757.html; congress.gov returned
    HTTP 403 to our requests.)
  - The only FY 2026 NPS budget document we found is the 6-page Budget in
    Brief chapter, which has no park-level figures:
    https://www.doi.gov/sites/default/files/documents/2025-06/fy26bibnps508.pdf
    (first Wayback capture 20250604050841).
- **Label.** Not available. We cannot show that it was ever published.

- **Where we looked.** The FY 2027 book (see item 3); the FY 2026 Budget in

  hearing of June 12, 2025, and the full FY 2026 joint explanatory statement.
- **Label.** Not available.

## 3. Park and Program Table and Park Visitation and Acreage section, FY 2027 book: no longer published

- **What it is.** The park-by-park table of park base FTE, total FTE and
  funding, and the section of visits and acreage by park.
- **Last published.** FY 2025 book (link above): "Park Visitation and
  Acreage" at Overview-22 (PDF page 28) and "Park and Program Table" at
  Overview-48 (PDF pages 54 to 74). Earlier books FY 2010 to FY 2024 have the
  park table too (see `research/datasets/20_budget.md`).
- **Where it is absent now.** FY 2027 book,
  https://www.doi.gov/sites/default/files/documents/2026-04/fy2027greenbooknps_0.pdf
  (HTTP 200, 219 pages, PDF created 2026-04-03; checked 2026-10-22).
  - Its table of contents (PDF pages 3 to 6) has no park table and no
    visitation section. The nearest item is "National Park System Units In
    Alphabetical Order" (PDF page 14), a list of names with no figures.
  - A text search finds no "Park and Program", no "Park Visitation" and no
    park base table. "Yosemite" appears only in the land acquisition project
    list (PDF pages 126 and 138), and "Yellowstone" only in a photo caption
    (PDF page 170).
  - A copy at the same address without `_0` (also HTTP 200, 219 pages) has the
    same text.
  - https://www.doi.gov/budget/appropriations/2027 lists only this one NPS
    book. Two other FY 2027 NPS files exist on doi.gov, and neither has park
    figures: the Budget in Brief chapter
    (https://www.doi.gov/sites/default/files/documents/2026-04/2027bibnps508.pdf)
    and a 21-page land acquisition project data sheet file
    (https://www.doi.gov/sites/default/files/documents/2026-05/nps-2027-request-pdss-508.pdf).
- **Evidence.** The FY 2025 and FY 2027 PDFs are cached in
  `data/raw/greenbooks/`. We found no statement from DOI or NPS about the
  change: not in the FY 2027 book (searched "no longer", "discontinu", "not
  included", "previously"), on the NPS budget page, on the DOI FY 2026 and
  FY 2027 budget pages, in the Budget in Brief pages, or in CRS IF13262 or
  R42757.
- **Label.** No longer published. These sections were in each book from FY
  2010 to FY 2025 and are not in the FY 2027 book.
